Frequently asked

Who pays for the ads?

You pay the platforms directly from your own ad account. Our fee covers strategy, setup and management only.

What budget do I need?

We recommend at least $1,000 per month in ad spend per platform to get useful data, but we will advise honestly if your budget is too small.

Do you guarantee results?

No ethical agency can guarantee ad results. We guarantee transparent reporting, clean tracking and steady optimisation.
