Frequently asked

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes 1-2 weeks. A business site usually takes 3-5 weeks depending on how quickly we receive content and feedback.

Will I own the website?

Yes. You own the design files, code and domain. We hand over everything at the end.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes, optional monthly care plans cover updates, backups, security checks and small edits.
