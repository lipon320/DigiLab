Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who we are

DigiLab ("we", "us") operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you use our contact or application forms, you provide details such as your name, email address, country, portfolio links and message. We use this information only to answer your enquiry or evaluate your application. Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form-handling service and are also recorded in a private spreadsheet (Google Sheets) that only the DigiLab team can access.

Advertising and cookies

This website displays advertising supplied by third-party advertising networks, including Adsterra. These networks may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show and measure ads and to limit how often you see them. Their handling of data is described in their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings or use browser privacy tools to limit tracking.

Analytics and logs

Our hosting provider may record standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested for security and performance purposes.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiries and applications only as long as needed to respond or to consider you for current and upcoming projects. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. Contact us through the contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please use the contact page.
