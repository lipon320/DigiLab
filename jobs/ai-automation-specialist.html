About the role

DigiLab is a remote-first digital agency serving clients in marketing, advertising, web design, AI, video and data. As a AI Automation Specialist, you will work with a distributed team of specialists, a named project lead and documented processes, so you can focus on doing excellent work instead of chasing context.

Design and build automations that connect tools and use AI to remove repetitive work for clients.

What you will do

  • Map client workflows and find automation opportunities
  • Build automations in Zapier, Make or n8n
  • Integrate AI models through APIs
  • Create error handling and monitoring
  • Document each automation for non-technical users
  • Train client teams to run them

What we are looking for

  • 2+ years building automations or integrations
  • Experience with Zapier, Make or n8n
  • Comfort working with APIs and JSON
  • Logical problem solving
  • Clear documentation habits

Nice to have

  • Python or JavaScript skills
  • Experience with vector databases
  • Experience in CRM or e-commerce automation

Tools you will use

ZapierMaken8nOpenAI APIAirtable

What we offer

  • 100% remote. Work from anywhere with a stable internet connection
  • Flexible hours with agreed overlap windows for client calls
  • Paid in USD via bank transfer, Wise or PayPal
  • Clear project briefs, templates and documented processes
  • Growth path: strong performers get more hours and lead roles
  • Learning budget for approved courses and tools (for ongoing roles)

Pay shown is an indicative USD range. The final rate depends on your skills, trial-task results and project scope.

Hiring process

  1. Apply online. Send your details and portfolio through the application form. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Portfolio review. We review your work and reply by email if your profile matches current or upcoming projects.
  3. Paid trial task. Shortlisted candidates complete a small, paid, real-world task so both sides can test the fit.
  4. Onboarding. You get access to our tools, SOPs and a project lead, then start on your first assignment.

Ready to apply?

It takes about five minutes. Share your portfolio and tell us why you are a great fit.
