About the role

DigiLab is a remote-first digital agency serving clients in marketing, advertising, web design, AI, video and data. As a Digital Marketing Strategist, you will work with a distributed team of specialists, a named project lead and documented processes, so you can focus on doing excellent work instead of chasing context.

Plan and run multi-channel growth strategies for DigiLab clients across e-commerce, SaaS and local businesses.

What you will do

  • Audit client funnels and build 90-day marketing roadmaps
  • Define KPIs, budgets and channel mix for each account
  • Brief designers, writers and ad specialists on campaign goals
  • Present monthly performance reviews to clients
  • Spot new growth opportunities and test them quickly
  • Document playbooks that the wider remote team can reuse

What we are looking for

  • 3+ years in digital marketing, agency or in-house
  • Strong grasp of funnels, attribution and analytics
  • Excellent written and spoken English
  • Comfortable managing several client accounts at once
  • Portfolio or case studies with measurable results

Nice to have

  • Experience with e-commerce or SaaS clients
  • Basic SQL or spreadsheet modelling
  • Experience leading small remote teams

Tools you will use

GA4HubSpotLooker StudioNotionSlack

What we offer

  • 100% remote. Work from anywhere with a stable internet connection
  • Flexible hours with agreed overlap windows for client calls
  • Paid in USD via bank transfer, Wise or PayPal
  • Clear project briefs, templates and documented processes
  • Growth path: strong performers get more hours and lead roles
  • Learning budget for approved courses and tools (for ongoing roles)

Pay shown is an indicative USD range. The final rate depends on your skills, trial-task results and project scope.

Hiring process

  1. Apply online. Send your details and portfolio through the application form. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Portfolio review. We review your work and reply by email if your profile matches current or upcoming projects.
  3. Paid trial task. Shortlisted candidates complete a small, paid, real-world task so both sides can test the fit.
  4. Onboarding. You get access to our tools, SOPs and a project lead, then start on your first assignment.

Ready to apply?

It takes about five minutes. Share your portfolio and tell us why you are a great fit.
