DigiLab connects businesses with skilled specialists around the world, so you get agency-level results without agency-level overhead.

Our story

Most small and mid-sized businesses need marketing, design, video, data and tech support, but cannot justify a full in-house team for each. Traditional agencies are expensive and slow, while freelance marketplaces leave you to manage everything alone.

DigiLab sits in the middle. We assemble a small team of specialists for your exact needs, put a project lead in charge, and keep everything transparent: scope, price in USD, progress and results.

Because our team works remotely, we can hire the best person for each job wherever they live, and pass the savings on in our pricing.

How our remote model works

  • Named project lead on every engagement
  • Specialists matched to your needs
  • Shared task board and weekly updates
  • Secure access with least privilege
  • NDA available on request
  • Written scope and USD quote before work starts
